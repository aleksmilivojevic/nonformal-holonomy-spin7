A construction by ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Ultra of a compact manifold with holonomy = Spin(7) which is also non-formal. 
The existence of such a manifold was widely expected after Martín-Merchán's tour de force construction of a non-formal G2 manifold, which disproved a longstanding conjecture that special holonomy compact manifolds are formal. 
This conjecture was in hindsight misguided by the red herring case of compact Kähler manifolds, where the salient property was in fact the del-delbar lemma. 
The existence of a complex manifold structure satisfying the del-delbar lemma suffices to establish formality with no assumption of compactness.
